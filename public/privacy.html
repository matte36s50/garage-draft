In short: we collect what it takes to run the game (your account, picks, predictions and chat) and a record of how you use it. This policy covers Bid Prix (the "game"), operated by [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us"). Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account. Your email address, the username you choose, and your password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a secure hash; we never see them.
  • Your game. The events (leagues) you join, the cars you draft and the price each was drafted at, your bonus-car predictions, your scores, ranks and results.
  • Chat. Messages you send in an event's chat.
  • How you use the game. While you're signed in we record events such as: screens you open, which cars were listed for you and in what order, which listings you open, cars you add or remove and why an add failed, predictions you open and submit, when you open chat and how long a message you sent was (never what it said), and how you sort the leaderboard. Each record has a time, your account, the event you had open, and a random id for your browser tab. Nothing is recorded while you're signed out.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and database providers log IP addresses, browser type and request times to keep the service running and secure.
  • On your device. The game stores your sign-in session, the event you last had open and the screen you were on in your browser's local storage, and a tab id in session storage. We use no advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To run the game: your account, drafting, scoring, leaderboards, results history, chat and notifications.
  • To keep it fair and safe: enforcing the rules, preventing cheating and abuse, and moderating chat.
  • To improve it: understanding where players get stuck or lose interest.
  • For market research: combining players' activity across many cars (for example, how many players looked at or drafted a model) to study interest in collector cars. This feeds our market indexes, such as the Market Interest Index. What we publish is aggregated across players and never identifies you.
